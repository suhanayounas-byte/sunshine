Jewellery-care tips

  • Put jewellery on last, after perfume and hair spray.
  • Remove before swimming, showering or workouts.
  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth after each wear.
  • Store pieces separately in a pouch or lined box.
  • Avoid long contact with water, oils and lotions.

Our story

Pretty, personal and a little bit festive.

Image placeholderimages/story.jpg

In Pakistan, jewellery arrives with the season: jhumkas for Eid mornings, pearls for a dawat, a ring stack for mehndi night. We love that sense of occasion.

SUNSHINE Jewellery is a feminine take on those traditions, with pieces styled for celebrations and for ordinary days. A locket is a gift that stays close. An initial ring says this one is mine.

We believe getting dressed is self-expression. Little treasures can make a day feel brighter.

FAQ

How long does delivery take?

3 to 5 working days in major cities and 5 to 8 elsewhere in Pakistan.

Can I exchange a piece?

Unworn pieces in original packaging can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Personalised items are excluded.

Is this real gold?

No. Pieces are fashion jewellery made from gold-plated alloy, with faux pearls, faux crystals and imitation kundan.

How do I care for my jewellery?

Keep it dry, put it on after perfume, store pieces separately and wipe with a soft cloth.

How do I order a personalised piece?

Preview your design in the Studio, then email or message us on Instagram with your design summary.
