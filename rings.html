Sizing guide

Find your size

Wrap a thin strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps and measure the length in millimetres. Match it to the circumference below. Sizes are approximate. A physical ring sizer gives the best fit.

Ring sizes with inner diameter and circumference
Ring size Inner diameter (mm) Circumference (mm)
5 15.7 49.3
6 16.5 51.8
7 17.3 54.4
8 18.2 57.0
9 18.9 59.5
